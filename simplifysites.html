Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Message me on WhatsApp.

How long does a project take?

Landing pages and resumes are usually delivered in 2–4 days. Online stores and booking apps typically take 4–7 days depending on scope and how quickly I get your feedback.

How much does it cost?

Pricing starts from the amounts listed above and depends on scope. You'll get an exact quote after a quick WhatsApp chat — no obligation to move forward.

Do I need to have designs or content ready?

No — most clients don't. I'll guide you through it step by step, from structure to copy to images.

Can you build an online store, not just a website?

Yes — product listings, cart, and checkout (via WhatsApp confirmation or an online payment link), ready to take orders from launch day.

What if I need changes after the site is live?

A short free adjustment window is included right after launch, and ongoing maintenance is available afterward — see the Website Maintenance service above.
